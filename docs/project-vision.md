# DHL Express Business Analytics Project Vision

## Project Title

DHL Express – Predictive Delivery Performance and Route Optimization

## Course

BAN6800 – Business Analytics

## Student

Akpevwe Peters

## Business Problem

The project focuses on identifying shipments at risk of late delivery early enough for operational intervention.

## Proposed Solution

The solution combines predictive analytics to estimate shipment delay risk with prescriptive analytics to support routing, capacity, and exception-management decisions.

## Project Approach

The project will use Python, SQL, pandas, scikit-learn, Power BI/Tableau, and appropriate cloud technologies.

## Governance

The solution will maintain human oversight and incorporate privacy, fairness, explainability, security, and model-monitoring controls.

## Pilot Target

The controlled pilot will target approximately a 10% reduction in avoidable late deliveries against baseline.
